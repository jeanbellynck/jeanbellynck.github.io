Research Interests

My academic interests lie in the fields of geometric topology and geometric group theory, with a focus on exploring the intricacies of 4-braids and their associated pseudo-Anosov homeomorphisms and Thurston's train tracks.

Additionally, I have a keen interest in the development of mathematical visualizations, using tools such as Java Processing or the Unity game engine to bring complex mathematical concepts to life through visual representations. A showcase project can be found below.

I have just started out as a researcher, so there really isn't much to say. If you still wish more information, my references offer insight into my current research focus.
